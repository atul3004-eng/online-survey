The Health Promotion Department at the Ministry of Public Health is conducting a mapping exercise to identify school health promotion programs and initiatives implemented in schools across Qatar.

FormatOne questionnaire per program
SectionsSeven guided sections
Estimated time10 - 15 minutes

Instructions

If you are implementing more than one school health promoting program or initiative, please complete a separate questionnaire for each program or initiative. Answer all questions in relation to the specific program, initiative, or activity being described. Use the questionnaire navigation buttons to move forward and backward, and submit only once you are comfortable with your responses.
For inquiries, contact HPS@MOPH.GOV.QA or 44077458. Your participation is highly appreciated.
