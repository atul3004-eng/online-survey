A mapping exercise to identify school health promotion programs and initiatives across the State of Qatar.

Background

This questionnaire is shared with relevant ministries, institutions, and MoPH departments to support coordination, alignment, and future planning. It collects information on existing or planned school health promotion programs, initiatives, or activities — and any related policies, guidelines, or documents.

Instructions
  • 1 If you are implementing more than one program or initiative, complete a separate questionnaire for each. Answer all questions only in relation to the specific program being described.
  • 2 Move forward and backward using the navigation buttons in the questionnaire — do not use your browser’s back and forward arrows.
  • 3 Once you are comfortable with all your responses, click Submit at the end of the questionnaire.
  • 4 This questionnaire contains seven sections, and every question should be answered.
  • 5 It takes about 10–15 minutes to complete.
